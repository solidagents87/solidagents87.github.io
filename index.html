How it works

  1. Map the work.

    We go through the repeat work in your business and pick one workflow worth handing to agents first.

  2. Build in your accounts.

    The agents live in your own Grok Bot workspace. You own the account, the billing and the work we deliver.

  3. Tune and write it down.

    We watch the agents work, fix what's off and write down how each one should run.

  4. Keep it running.

    Optional monthly upkeep as your business changes: new jobs, new tools, better rules.

What you get

  • One mapped workflow, built in your own accounts.
  • Agents with written jobs and clear approval gates.
  • Changes tested before they go live.
  • Instructions, routines, runbooks and files you keep.
  • A monthly scorecard of work done, human time spent and running cost. Anything not measured is marked "not measured".
