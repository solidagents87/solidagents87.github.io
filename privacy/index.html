Privacy notice

Last updated November 27, 2026.

This website

solidagents.net is a static website. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, ad trackers or forms. Our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security and to run the service.

Email

If you email us@solidagents.net, we get your name, email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it only to reply to you and to follow up on what you asked about. Our email runs on Google Workspace. Our AI agents may help sort email and draft replies; a person approves anything we send.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it except with the service providers we use to run our email and website, or when the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting

We keep email only as long as we need it for the conversation and our normal business records. Email us@solidagents.net to ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted.

Client work

Agent teams we set up for clients run in the client's own accounts, under the client's own provider terms. Each engagement's contract covers how client data is handled.

Changes

If this notice changes, we'll update the date at the top.
