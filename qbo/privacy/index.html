Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Solid Strategic Services LLC ("we", "us"), 3101 Cobb Pkwy, Suite 124, Atlanta, GA 30339, USA, handles data in Solid Strategic Bookkeeping (the "App"), a private internal tool. The App only connects to QuickBooks Online companies owned by us or our affiliated businesses.

Data the App accesses

When a company administrator connects a QuickBooks Online company, the App uses the QuickBooks Online Accounting API (scope com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting) to read that company's accounting data. This can include company information, accounts, classes, vendors, customers, transactions and financial reports. Some of this data may name people, such as vendors, customers or employees recorded in the books. When an authorized person approves it, the App also writes accounting entries, such as journal entries and purchases, to that company.

How we use it

We use the data only to keep the connected companies' books: to look up accounts, prepare and check entries, post approved entries and produce internal reports. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share the data with third parties. It is used only by our own staff and the automated assistants we operate for the businesses whose books they are. We would disclose data only if required by law.

Storage and security

The OAuth tokens that authorize the connection are stored on an access-controlled Linux host operated by us, in files readable only by the account that runs the App. They are never written to logs, source code or chat. All communication with Intuit uses HTTPS (TLS). Accounting data the App reads is held only as long as needed for the task or report it was read for.

Cookies and analytics

The App and these web pages use no cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or third-party scripts.

Retention and deletion

When a company is disconnected, we revoke the App's tokens with Intuit and delete the stored tokens for that company from our host. If a company disconnects the App from inside QuickBooks Online, the tokens stop working, and we delete them from our host. Accounting records already posted to QuickBooks remain in that company's QuickBooks file, which is controlled by the company and by Intuit's own policies. To ask us to delete anything we hold, email us.
