Connect or reconnect QuickBooks Online

Connections are started by Solid Strategic Services LLC's own team. They are not self-service.

  1. An authorized operator starts the connection from our host. This opens Intuit's standard sign-in and consent screen.
  2. An administrator of the QuickBooks Online company signs in to Intuit, chooses the correct company and approves access for Solid Strategic Bookkeeping.
  3. The App confirms the company name before using the connection.

Reconnect the same way if a connection expires or is disconnected. If you need a company connected or reconnected, email us@solidagents.net.
